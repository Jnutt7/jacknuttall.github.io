About

I'm a mechanical engineering student at USC, now working toward a combined Master's in Product Development Engineering. Most recently I interned at DZYNE Technologies / Ondas Sentinel in Irvine, designing a lightweight launcher for expendable UAVs from concept through early production. Before that, I spent a year and a half at Abbott Laboratories building inspection tooling and rework processes for leadless pacemakers.

Outside of internships, I lead hands-on builds through USC Makers and my senior capstone — right now that means managing a four-person team designing a UAV that automatically repositions its own battery to correct for shifting center of gravity mid-flight. I like problems where a mechanism has to work precisely and repeatably under real constraints, not just once on a bench.

I'm originally from Narragansett, Rhode Island, and I'm still getting used to — and really enjoying — life on the west coast. Outside of engineering, I'm usually listening to blues and R&B, lifting weights, at the beach, fishing, or spending time with my church community.

Music is my other outlet: I write and record my own songs, play guitar, sing, and do my own audio engineering. You can find what I've put out so far on Spotify.
