SOL'Y CLEAN SOLAR · September 2023 — May 2024 · Team of 6

Self-Cleaning Solar Panel System

Mechanical Team Lead · USC Makers

Dust buildup on solar panels ("soiling") costs the industry billions in lost output every year. Our team built a self-tracking, self-cleaning panel platform to fight that loss without the water use of manual cleaning — I led the mechanical design of the panel support, tracking base, and cleaning carriage.

  • Designed the "Sol'y Roller" cleaning carriage in SolidWorks — a microfiber-roller system on SBR linear guide rails, driven by a stepper and 12V DC motor through a 3D-printed rack and pinion.
  • Moved the cleaner away from gravity-fed, water-based cleaning to a dry brushing method suited to hot, dusty climates like LA, avoiding evaporation losses entirely.
  • Built a lazy-susan-style rotating base with a center-mounted CIM motor and planetary gearbox so the panel could track the sun.
  • Sized and mounted dual linear actuators for panel tilt, paired with a BLDC motor for base rotation.
  • Iterated the panel support and actuator mounting after early wobble issues, balancing structural rigidity against added system weight.
