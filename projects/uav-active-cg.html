Active CG Optimization System for Small UAVs

Project Manager

A fixed-wing UAV test platform that autonomously repositions its own battery to compensate for payload-induced shifts in center of gravity, keeping the aircraft trimmed and stable as its load changes mid-flight.

4
TEAM MEMBERS
10–15%
TARGET STATIC MARGIN
60
RUN TEST MATRIX
  • Developing mass-moment, neutral-point, and static-margin calculations to determine required battery travel while maintaining a 10–15% longitudinal static margin.
  • Architecting a closed-loop control system using IMU, elevator-angle, and carriage-position feedback to minimize steady-state elevator deflection at a commanded pitch attitude.
  • Designed a 60-run wind-tunnel test matrix comparing fixed- and active-CG configurations across elevator deflection, drag, pitch error, settling time, and actuator activity.
