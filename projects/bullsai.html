BULLSAI · August 2024 — May 2025 · Team of 6

Autonomous Robotic Dart Player

Project Manager · USC Makers

A self-guided robotic arm that throws darts and scores itself. I led the six-person team while driving the throwing-arm kinematics and mechanical design: a stepper-driven arm releases a custom dart at a calculated speed and angle, and a computer-vision system reads the landing position against the board.

90%
BULLSEYE HIT RATE
15
3D-PRINTED COMPONENTS
  • Modeled dart trajectories in MATLAB to optimize release parameters, achieving a 90% bullseye hit rate during testing.
  • Designed and 3D-printed a 15-component assembly in SolidWorks, refining component interfaces to improve fit.
  • Swapped a 20-step encoder for a 360-step encoder after the original couldn't resolve arm velocity accurately enough.
  • Replaced a servo gripper with a solenoid-and-reed-switch release after the servo proved too slow to hold and fire reliably.
  • Designed a custom 3D-printed dart carrying an IR LED and coin-cell battery for the computer-vision system to track.
  • Built the scoring pipeline around image filtering and ArUco tag detection to normalize the board and read the dart's position.
  • Developed a structured project roadmap in Notion, resulting in timely completion for the semester showcase.
