AUTOFRET · August 2025 — May 2026

Automated Fretting System for Acoustic Guitar

Lead Mechanical Designer

A robotic fretting system that clamps onto a standard acoustic guitar neck and mechanically presses each string at the correct fret to form chords — essentially a hands-free chord-forming assistant. I designed the full mechanical system: the neck-mounted frame, the linear drive for each string, and the fretting mechanism itself.

12
STEPPER MOTORS

STRINGS ACTUATED
2020
RAIL PROFILE
  • Designed the full neck-interface system in SolidWorks: 2020 aluminum-extrusion rails, 3D-printed motor housings, and clamps that mount the assembly directly to the guitar neck without modifying the instrument.
  • Built six linear carriages — one per string — driven by a tensioned belt-and-pulley system, each powered by its own stepper motor for independent positioning along the fretboard.
  • Designed six rack-and-pinion "finger" mechanisms, one per string, each driven by its own stepper motor to press the string down at the correct fret.
  • Integrated all 12 stepper motors (6 linear-position + 6 fretting) into a single frame compact enough to mount on and play a standard acoustic guitar, demoed live at a USC Viterbi showcase.
